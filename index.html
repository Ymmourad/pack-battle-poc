How Pack Battles works

A quick tour of the games, features and how a battle plays out.

🎯How it works

Join a battle from the lobby (or create your own). Every round you pay an entry fee and rip the same packs as everyone else. When the round ends, the player with the highest total card value takes everything in that round. After all rounds, whoever has taken home the most bankroll wins.

Your bankroll is virtual — use + Recharge (top right) to add $1,000. Battles come in three categories: Pokémon TCG, One Piece and Sports cards.

🎮Battle modes

Default

Everyone rips the same packs each round. Highest total value takes all cards from that round.

e.g. you rip $420 vs HoloHunter's $310 → you take home $730 this round.
Inverse

A random heat multiplier (0.5×–3×) is rolled each round and applies to every pull. The lowest total takes everything — don't get lucky!

e.g. a ×2 roll doubles the stakes; the player who rips less wins.
Sharing

Lower risk: the round's total card value is split evenly between all players who opened.

e.g. 4 players rip $1,500 total → everyone takes home ~$375.

🏠Lobby features

  • Filter & sort — narrow by mode or player count, sort by price.
  • Details on any row — click a battle (or its Details button) for the full loot table: drop rates, value ranges, example cards and expected value per pack.
  • + Create Battle — pick category, mode, players, rounds and packs; start instantly vs bots or open an invite room with a shareable code.

⚖In the battle room

  • Provably Fair Data (top left) shows the battle seed and each round's digest. Every card is derived from hash(seed + nonce), so no outcome can be altered after the fact.
  • Pace controls — 1× / 2× / Turbo speed, Pause and Sound toggle sit under the arena.
  • Bankroll matters — your entry is charged every round; if you can't cover the next round, you forfeit the battle.
